AI账单 / AiBill · PRIVACY

隐私政策

本政策适用于 AI账单 iOS 应用。最近更新：2026 年 10 月 28 日。

本地账单与付款方式

订阅名称、金额、周期、付款方式备注、扣费日期和自定义图标默认保存在设备上的应用私有空间。应用不会连接银行账户，也不会读取真实银行卡交易。

iCloud 同步（由你选择）

开启自动 iCloud 同步或手动同步后，账本备份会保存在你个人 Apple 账户的 CloudKit 私有数据库中。该数据库由 Apple 管理，开发者不通过自有服务器接收账本内容。关闭同步不会自动删除已经保存的 iCloud 数据。

App Store 搜索与图标

只有当你主动使用“App 图标”搜索时，应用才会将你输入的搜索词及选定的 App Store 地区发送至 Apple 的 iTunes Search API，并从 Apple 提供的网址下载你选择的图标。这些请求受 Apple 自身隐私政策约束。

Pro 购买与恢复

购买、续订、退款和恢复购买由 Apple StoreKit 与 App Store 处理。本应用读取 Apple 验证后的权益状态，不要求你提供银行卡、完整付款凭据或 Apple 账户密码。

本机提醒与小组件

开启扣费提醒后，通知由设备本地安排。桌面小组件只使用与你的 App 共享的本机数据。可以在应用设置及系统设置中管理提醒权限。

数据管理与保留

你可以在应用内修改或删除订阅、导出 JSON 备份，或卸载应用删除本机应用数据。若使用过 iCloud，同步数据可能继续保留在个人 iCloud 空间；可通过 Apple 的 iCloud 储存空间与账户管理渠道管理。删除本机数据或关闭同步不等于删除 iCloud 副本。

跟踪与开发者服务器

当前版本没有集成第三方广告网络、行为分析或跨 App 跟踪 SDK，也没有用于收集私人账本的开发者服务器。系统、App Store 和 Apple 的服务可能按各自政策处理诊断、交易和网络数据。

与开发者联系

如对本政策有疑问，请使用该应用 App Store 产品页面公开的开发者联系方式。

Privacy Policy

This policy applies to the AI账单 iOS app. Last updated: October 28, 2026.

Local ledger and payment methods

Subscription names, amounts, billing cycles, payment-method labels, renewal dates, and custom icons are stored in the app's private storage on your device by default. The app does not connect to banks or read your bank-card transactions.

iCloud sync (your choice)

If you enable automatic iCloud sync or manually start a sync, a copy of your ledger is saved in your Apple account's private CloudKit database. Apple operates this storage; the app developer does not receive your ledger contents on an independent server. Turning off sync does not automatically delete copies already saved in iCloud.

App Store search and artwork

Only when you choose to search for app artwork does the app send your search term and selected storefront region to Apple's iTunes Search API. If you select an icon, the app downloads artwork from an Apple-hosted URL. These requests are also subject to Apple's own privacy practices.

Pro purchases and restoration

Apple's StoreKit and App Store process purchases, renewals, refunds, and purchase restoration. The app reads verified entitlement status from Apple; it does not ask you to provide bank-card details, complete payment credentials, or your Apple Account password.

On-device reminders and widgets

If enabled, billing reminders are scheduled locally on your device. Home Screen widgets use on-device data shared with the app. You can manage reminder settings in the app and notification permissions in iOS Settings.

Managing and retaining data

You can edit or delete entries, export a JSON backup, or uninstall the app to remove its local app data. If you have used iCloud, synchronized copies may remain in your personal iCloud storage and can be managed through Apple's iCloud storage and account controls. Removing local data or disabling sync does not automatically delete an iCloud copy.

Tracking and developer servers

The current version includes no third-party ad network, behavior analytics, or cross-app tracking SDK, and no developer-operated server that receives private ledgers. iOS, the App Store, and Apple's services may handle diagnostic, transaction, and network data under their own policies.

Contacting the developer

For questions about this policy, use the developer contact channel shown on the app's App Store product page.
