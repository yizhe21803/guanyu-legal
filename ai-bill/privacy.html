Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This policy covers the AI账单 (AiBill) iOS app. AI账单 is a subscription and payment-reminder organizer; it is not a banking app.

1. Information you enter

Subscription names, amounts, currencies, renewal periods, billing dates, payment-method notes, custom icons, and preferences are stored in the app's private local storage on your device by default. The app does not connect to your bank account or automatically read card transactions.

2. Optional iCloud synchronization

If you enable automatic iCloud sync or initiate a manual sync, a copy of your subscription ledger is stored in the private CloudKit database of your own Apple Account. Apple operates the iCloud service. The app does not transmit your ledger contents to a server operated by the AI账单 developer. Switching off synchronization does not automatically delete data already saved in iCloud.

3. Optional App Store icon search

When you choose to search for an app icon, the search terms you enter and the selected App Store country code are transmitted to Apple's iTunes Search API. If you select a result, its artwork is downloaded from Apple's image service. Those requests are subject to Apple's own privacy practices. Icon searches are initiated by you, not performed continuously in the background.

4. In-app purchases

Pro subscriptions, one-time purchases, renewals, refunds, and purchase restoration are processed by Apple using StoreKit and the App Store. AI账单 checks verified purchase entitlements to enable Pro features. The developer does not ask for your Apple Account password, full card number, or payment credentials.

5. Reminders and widgets

If enabled, billing reminders are scheduled as local iOS notifications. Home-screen widgets use on-device data shared between the app and its own extension. You can manage reminder permission in the app and iOS Settings.

6. Retention and deletion

You can edit or delete records in the app and export a JSON backup. Removing the app may remove its local data, so consider exporting a backup first. If iCloud synchronization was used, a private iCloud copy may remain after you turn off sync or delete the app; manage app-related cloud storage through your Apple Account's iCloud settings. Deleting local data alone does not necessarily delete a cloud copy.

7. Analytics and tracking

The current app build does not contain third-party advertising, behavioral analytics, or cross-app tracking SDKs, and the developer does not operate a telemetry service that collects your private ledger. Apple and its services may independently process network, diagnostic, account, and transaction information under Apple's policies.

8. Contact and changes

For questions about this policy, contact GUANYU LAB at 30501813@qq.com, or see our support information. Material changes to this policy will be reflected in an updated version of this page.

隐私政策

最近更新：2026 年 10 月 28 日

本政策适用于 AI账单 iOS 应用。AI账单是订阅及扣费提醒管理工具，不是银行服务应用。

一、您输入的账单数据

订阅名称、金额、货币、周期、扣费日期、付款方式备注、自定义图标及个人偏好，默认存储在设备上的应用私有空间内。应用不会连接银行账户，也不会自动读取银行卡交易。

二、可选的 iCloud 同步

开启自动 iCloud 同步或手动发起同步后，您的订阅账本备份将保存在您个人 Apple 账户的 CloudKit 私有数据库中。iCloud 由 Apple 提供，AI账单开发者不通过自有服务器接收账本内容。关闭同步不会自动删除已保存在 iCloud 中的数据。

三、可选的 App Store 图标搜索

当您主动搜索 App 图标时，输入的搜索词和选定的 App Store 地区代码会发送给 Apple 的 iTunes Search API。选中结果后，应用会从 Apple 图像服务下载对应图标。这些请求受 Apple 自身的隐私政策约束。应用不会在后台持续发送图标搜索请求。

四、Pro 购买与恢复

Pro 订阅、一次性买断、续订、退款及恢复购买由 Apple 通过 StoreKit 和 App Store 处理。AI账单仅检查经过验证的购买权益，用于解锁 Pro 功能。开发者不要求您提供 Apple 账户密码、完整银行卡号或支付凭证。

五、本机提醒和小组件

开启扣费提醒后，通知由 iOS 在本机安排。桌面小组件使用与本应用扩展共享的本机数据。您可以在应用和 iOS 设置中管理提醒权限。

六、数据保留和删除

您可以在应用中编辑或删除记录，并导出 JSON 备份。卸载应用可能移除本机数据，请事先考虑导出备份。如果使用过 iCloud 同步，关闭同步或卸载应用后，私人 iCloud 副本可能仍会保留；可通过 Apple 账户的 iCloud 储存空间管理相关数据。仅删除本地数据不一定会删除云端副本。

七、分析与跟踪

当前版本没有集成第三方广告网络、行为分析或跨 App 跟踪 SDK，开发者亦未运营收集私人账本内容的遥测服务器。Apple 及其服务可能根据各自政策处理网络、诊断、账户和交易相关信息。
