AI账单 / AiBill — Support

Restore Pro purchases

Open AI账单, go to Settings → Pro, and choose Restore Purchases. Ensure you use the Apple Account used for the original purchase. Your subscription management and refunds are provided by Apple.

iCloud sync

Sign into your Apple Account and enable iCloud. In AI账单 Settings, turn on automatic iCloud sync or start a manual sync. Back up your ledger before switching accounts. Switching iCloud accounts automatically pauses sync to protect existing local data.

Data and privacy

View the Privacy Policy. To export your data, use the JSON backup feature in AI账单. To manage a previously stored private iCloud backup, use Apple Account → iCloud storage settings.

恢复 Pro 购买

打开 AI账单 → 设置 → Pro → 恢复购买。请使用原购买对应的 Apple 账户。订阅管理和退款由 Apple 提供。

iCloud 同步

请先登录 Apple 账户并启用 iCloud，再到 AI账单设置中开启自动同步或手动同步。切换账户前请导出账本备份。为保护本机数据，切换 iCloud 账户会暂停同步。

数据与隐私

阅读隐私政策。应用支持导出 JSON 账本备份。曾同步至私人 iCloud 的数据，请通过 Apple 账户的 iCloud 储存空间进行管理。
