一亩羊 Woolacre — 技术支持

恢复购买

打开一亩羊 → 我的牧场 → 右上角设置 → 恢复购买。请使用当初购买完整版的 Apple 账户。完整版支持家人共享；退款由 Apple 处理。

小组件没更新

小组件会按清晨、白天、傍晚、夜里自动换图。刚换了牧场或摆设，可以到 设置 → 小组件 →「把现在的牧场放上去」马上更新。

环境声没有声音

请检查静音开关和媒体音量；设置里的「声音」开关也要打开。专注和睡前的环境声锁屏后会继续播放。

换了手机，牧场没了

牧场只保存在原来的设备上，不会同步，也没有云备份。重新安装或换设备后会从新的牧场开始；完整版可以通过「恢复购买」找回。

Restore purchases

Open Woolacre → My Pasture → Settings (top right) → Restore Purchases, using the Apple Account that bought the full version. Family Sharing is supported; refunds are handled by Apple.

Widgets not updating

Widgets switch automatically between morning, day, evening and night. After changing your pasture, use Settings → Widgets → "Put the current pasture on it" to refresh right away.

No ambient sound

Check the silent switch and media volume, and make sure Sound is on in Settings. Ambient sound keeps playing on the lock screen during focus and bedtime.

New device, pasture is gone

Your pasture is stored only on the original device and is not synced. Purchases can be restored with Restore Purchases.
