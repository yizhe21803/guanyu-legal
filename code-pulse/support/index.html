Purchases / 购买与恢复

Purchases are processed by Apple. To restore an eligible purchase, open the Pro page in the app and select Restore Purchases. Refund requests and subscription management are handled through your Apple account and the App Store.

Alerts / 通知提醒

To receive reset alerts, allow notifications in iOS Settings and enable the relevant alert options in the app. Delivery depends on iOS permissions and network conditions.

如需接收重置提醒，请在 iOS 设置中开启通知权限，并检查 App 内相应提醒开关。实际送达受系统权限及网络环境影响。
