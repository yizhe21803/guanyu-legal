Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 2026-09-25

CODE PULSE is a personal signal and reset-rhythm app from GUANYU LAB. This policy explains the information the app processes to provide its features.

Information processed

The app creates a random installation identifier and sends it to our API with app version, client platform, language, and time-zone information. We use this information to associate requests with an installation and return the app's content.

If you allow notifications, Apple provides the app with an APNs device token. The app sends that token, its notification environment, and notification authorization status to our API so notification delivery can be configured. You can change notification permission in iOS Settings.

When the app contacts our API, operational records may include request identifiers, endpoint and method, response status, timing, cache status, and retry information. These records are used to operate and diagnose the service.

CODE PULSE uses Apple StoreKit for in-app purchases and reads transaction and entitlement status to determine Pro access. Payment card and billing information is handled by Apple; the app does not receive your payment card number.

Information we do not use for advertising

Our current iOS implementation does not include third-party advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs. It does not request access to your location, contacts, photos, or camera. The app does not require account registration or provide social features.

Service providers

API hosting and delivery infrastructure may process the technical information described above on GUANYU LAB's behalf. Apple processes App Store transactions and notification delivery under Apple's own terms and privacy policy.

Retention and choices

We retain operational records and installation information only for as long as reasonably needed to operate, secure, and improve the service. You may disable notifications in iOS Settings. For privacy questions or a request concerning information associated with an installation, contact us at 30501813@qq.com.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The date above indicates when this version was last updated.
