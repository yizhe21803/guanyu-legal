隐私政策

本政策适用于独立开发的「归途」iOS 公益寻亲工具。最近更新：2026 年 11 月 17 日。本应用不代表公安机关或任何原始资料发布机构。

一、历史公开档案与未成年人信息

应用安装包包含从第三方原发布渠道整理的历史公开寻亲档案，包括可能涉及未成年人的姓名、性别、出生或失踪年份、失踪地区、特征描述，以及原渠道曾公开的部分照片。这些资料作为只读历史快照供检索，不等于当前仍在寻找，也不代表资料已获实时核验。儿童个人信息、图片的使用及删除须以合法授权和最新更正为前提。

二、使用目的和地图显示

档案用于本机查看、姓名或编号检索、分类浏览、收藏和省市级分布概览。地图标记采用行政区中心坐标，不是儿童的准确失踪地点，也不用于追踪、识别或确定任何儿童的实时位置。应用没有人脸识别功能。

三、本机偏好和收藏

当前版本无需注册账号。所选择的显示模式和收藏档案编号保存在本机应用存储；开发者不通过自有服务器同步或接收这些收藏。卸载应用通常会移除本机偏好和收藏，但内置历史公开档案是应用安装包的一部分，需要通过后续版本更新或停止分发进行更正。

四、可选定位与 Apple 地图

仅当您主动点击地图的「定位」按钮时，应用才会通过 iOS 请求定位权限，用于临时将地图移至附近区域。当前代码不将用户定位上传到开发者服务器，不持久保存定位历史。地图底图和地图网络请求由 Apple MapKit 提供，Apple 可能按其政策处理相关网络信息及地图请求；可在 iOS「设置」中随时关闭定位权限。

五、外部网站和开发者联系

选择原始发布来源、App Store、开发者社交主页等外部链接时，相应第三方网站或 Apple 服务可能按照其隐私政策处理网络信息。通过邮箱联系开发者时，您主动发送的邮箱地址及邮件内容会交由相关邮件服务提供商处理，并用于回复问题、纠错或删除请求。请勿通过普通反馈邮件提交儿童精确位置、证件、家庭联系方式或新的敏感线索；紧急失踪线索请直接联系警方或原发布机构。

六、资料纠错、下架与个人权利

如您是档案当事人、监护人或合法权利代表，可联系 30501813@qq.com，请提供必要的公开登记编号以及更正或撤回理由；请尽量避免提交额外的敏感个人资料。我们会核查并处理合理的更正或停止展示请求。由于当前档案为离线安装包，已安装版本不会自动实时删除对应档案；必要时须更新 App 或调整发布。本应用无法代替原发布方修改其网站记录。

七、留存、跟踪与安全

显示设置和收藏保留在本机，直到您更改、清除应用数据或卸载；安装包中的历史档案保留到软件更新移除对应数据。支持邮件会在处理反馈和必要法律义务所需期间保留，您可以请求删除。当前构建未集成第三方广告、行为分析或跨应用跟踪 SDK，也没有开发者运营的个人定位收集接口；Apple、邮件服务商及外部网站仍可能按其各自政策处理相关数据。

八、联系与变更

隐私或资料撤回问题：30501813@qq.com。若数据处理方式或应用功能发生重大变化，将同步更新 App 内和正式发布网站上的政策文本。是否具备发布权限及资料使用授权，应在公开上线前进一步核验。

Privacy Policy

This policy covers GuiTu, an independently developed iOS public-interest missing-person information viewer. Updated November 17, 2026. This app does not represent law enforcement or the original publishers.

1. Public historical records and minors

The app bundles historical records compiled from external public sources. Records may include minors' names, gender, birth or missing years, regions, descriptions, and photos previously displayed by the original publishers. Records are read-only historical snapshots, not a verified live list. Lawful permission, current corrections and removal requests remain essential.

2. Purpose and map display

Records support local browsing, searches, categories, bookmarks and regional maps. Map markers are administrative-area centers, NOT a child's precise missing location or present position. The app does not include facial recognition or child tracking.

3. On-device preferences and bookmarks

No account is required in this version. Theme settings and bookmarked record IDs are stored locally and are not synced to a developer-operated server. Uninstalling normally removes local preferences and bookmarks. Historical records are bundled with the installed app, so corrections require an app update or distribution changes.

4. Optional device location and Apple Maps

Only when you tap the map's location button will the app request iOS location permission to move the map near you. This version does not send your location to the developer's server or retain location history. Apple MapKit supplies map tiles and may process map-related network data under Apple's policies. Permission can be revoked in iOS Settings.

5. External sites and developer contact

Opening source sites, the App Store or social profiles may involve third-party processing under their policies. If you email the developer, your address and message are processed by the relevant mail providers and used to respond to feedback, correction or deletion requests. Please do not send minors' exact locations, identity documents, private contacts or sensitive tips by ordinary support email; contact police or the original source for urgent information.

6. Corrections, removal and data rights

Data subjects, guardians or authorized rights holders may contact 30501813@qq.com with the public record ID and the correction or removal reason; avoid sharing further sensitive data. Reasonable correction and removal requests will be reviewed. Because records are bundled offline, installed versions cannot be remotely erased in real time; app updates or distribution actions may be required. This app cannot alter the original publisher's records.

7. Retention, tracking and safety

Settings and bookmarks remain on-device until changed or app data is removed. Bundled historical records remain until updated out of the app. Support email may be retained as necessary for requests and legal obligations; you can request deletion. This build has no third-party advertising, behavioral analytics or cross-app tracking SDK and no developer-run location collection endpoint. Apple, mail providers and external sites may process data under their own policies.

8. Contact and updates

Privacy and removal requests: 30501813@qq.com. Material changes to data processing or features will be reflected in the app and the published policy. Rights to redistribute the source materials must be verified before public distribution.
