隐私政策

本政策适用于独立开发的「宝贝归途」iOS 公益寻亲工具。最近更新：2026 年 11 月 17 日。本应用不代表公安机关或任何原始资料发布机构。

一、历史公开档案与未成年人信息

本应用不在安装包内预置儿童档案或真实照片。应用通过独立同步服务定时从原发布渠道读取仍公开的登记页，包含姓名、编号、性别、出生或失踪年份、地区和特征信息；公开照片在实际展示时从原站按需获取。同步公开并不等于已验证仍在失踪。我们会限制过期缓存并处理删除、更正和撤回请求。

二、使用目的和地图显示

档案用于本机查看、姓名或编号检索、分类浏览、收藏和省市级分布概览。地图标记采用行政区中心坐标，不是儿童的准确失踪地点，也不用于追踪、识别或确定任何儿童的实时位置。应用没有人脸识别功能。

三、本机偏好和收藏

当前版本无需注册账号。显示模式和收藏的公开档案编号保存在设备本机，不上传给开发者服务器。公开档案索引从在线数据服务读取，并在本机缓存至其有效期届满；过期数据不再展示。撤回记录会在成功同步新版完整清单后从列表和地图中移除，原始照片只按需加载到临时内存缓存。

四、可选定位与 Apple 地图

仅当您主动点击地图的「定位」按钮时，应用才会通过 iOS 请求定位权限，用于临时将地图移至附近区域。当前代码不将用户定位上传到开发者服务器，不持久保存定位历史。地图底图和地图网络请求由 Apple MapKit 提供，Apple 可能按其政策处理相关网络信息及地图请求；可在 iOS「设置」中随时关闭定位权限。

五、外部网站和开发者联系

选择原始发布来源、App Store、开发者社交主页等外部链接时，相应第三方网站或 Apple 服务可能按照其隐私政策处理网络信息。通过邮箱联系开发者时，您主动发送的邮箱地址及邮件内容会交由相关邮件服务提供商处理，并用于回复问题、纠错或删除请求。请勿通过普通反馈邮件提交儿童精确位置、证件、家庭联系方式或新的敏感线索；紧急失踪线索请直接联系警方或原发布机构。

六、资料纠错、下架与个人权利

如您是档案当事人、监护人或合法权利代表，可联系 30501813@qq.com，请提供必要的公开登记编号及更正或撤回理由，避免额外敏感信息。已从原站公开列表移除的资料会在下一次完整同步后从客户端展示中消失；无网络时本地缓存超过有效期会自动失效。由于同步和客户端联网需要时间，撤回并非所有设备瞬时完成；对于紧急撤回可人工暂停数据接口。本应用不能代替原发布方修改原站资料。

七、留存、跟踪与安全

显示设置和收藏编号保留在本机直到更改或卸载。在线公开档案索引仅暂存在应用缓存目录，依发布数据中的有效期自动失效；照片仅在查看时按需取用并保存在有内存上限的临时缓存。支持邮件按处理需求保留，您可申请删除。当前应用未集成第三方广告、行为分析或跨应用跟踪 SDK；Apple、邮件服务、原站与数据托管方可能按其自身政策处理网络请求。

八、联系与变更

隐私或资料撤回问题：30501813@qq.com。若数据处理方式或应用功能发生重大变化，将同步更新 App 内和正式发布网站上的政策文本。是否具备发布权限及资料使用授权，应在公开上线前进一步核验。

Privacy Policy

This policy covers GuiTu, an independently developed iOS public-interest missing-person information viewer. Updated November 17, 2026. This app does not represent law enforcement or the original publishers.

1. Public historical records and minors

The app does not bundle children's records or authentic photos. It fetches a periodically refreshed, source-linked index from an independent synchronizer that reads currently public source pages. The index may include names, public IDs, gender, dates, regions and descriptions of minors. Photos are loaded on demand from the original source. Public listing is not proof of an active missing-person case. Cached data expires; requests for correction or removal are supported.

2. Purpose and map display

Records support local browsing, searches, categories, bookmarks and regional maps. Map markers are administrative-area centers, NOT a child's precise missing location or present position. The app does not include facial recognition or child tracking.

3. On-device preferences and bookmarks

No account is required. Theme preferences and bookmarked public record IDs stay on device; they are not uploaded to the developer's server. The online index may be stored in a time-limited device cache, and expired data is hidden. Records removed from a successfully synchronized complete index disappear from lists and maps. Original photos are loaded on demand into an ephemeral memory cache.

4. Optional device location and Apple Maps

Only when you tap the map's location button will the app request iOS location permission to move the map near you. This version does not send your location to the developer's server or retain location history. Apple MapKit supplies map tiles and may process map-related network data under Apple's policies. Permission can be revoked in iOS Settings.

5. External sites and developer contact

Opening source sites, the App Store or social profiles may involve third-party processing under their policies. If you email the developer, your address and message are processed by the relevant mail providers and used to respond to feedback, correction or deletion requests. Please do not send minors' exact locations, identity documents, private contacts or sensitive tips by ordinary support email; contact police or the original source for urgent information.

6. Corrections, removal and data rights

Data subjects, guardians or rights holders may request correction/removal via 30501813@qq.com with the public record ID; please avoid extra sensitive information. Records no longer published by the original site are removed from the client's display after its next successful full sync; offline cached indexes expire automatically. Removal is not instantaneous on all devices. For urgent removal, the feed can be suspended. This app cannot alter the publisher's own site.

7. Retention, tracking and safety

Theme settings and public record IDs remain on device until changed or the app is removed. Online indexes are cached only until their declared expiry; photos are downloaded on demand into a bounded, temporary memory cache. Support emails may be retained as necessary and deleted on request. This build has no advertising, behavior analytics or cross-app tracking SDK. Apple, the source website, mail providers and the feed host may process network requests under their own policies.

8. Contact and updates

Privacy and removal requests: 30501813@qq.com. Material changes to data processing or features will be reflected in the app and the published policy. Rights to redistribute the source materials must be verified before public distribution.
